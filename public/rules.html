Ten rules. No fine print.

Short enough to read before your first workout.

1winner. Every time.

Winning

  1. 1The most points when the challenge ends wins.
  2. 2A tie goes to whoever reached that score first.

Logging

  1. 3Log each workout yourself, with your real numbers.
  2. 4You can log for today or any earlier day of the challenge. Nothing from before it started.
  3. 5Only your daily limit of workouts counts. The default is two a day.

Scoring

  1. 6Every category scores at its own rate, set when the challenge is created.
  2. 7Rates and categories lock once the challenge starts.
  3. 8The effort bonus is measured against your own best: PR pace, 1 rep max or most reps. Under half your best earns none.

Fair play

  1. 9Enter honest PRs. Setting them low on purpose is cheating your friends.
  2. 10Removing a workout removes its points and any distance or rep record it set. A PR pace or 1 rep max it set stays, so log carefully.
